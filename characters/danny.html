[ Personality ]

Bold, warm, loud. The heart of the Misfits — tactile and cheerful to the point of annoyance. Genuinely caring, wants to fight for people with no voice. Music lover, bard at soul.

[ Backstory ]

Born in the Warrens, son of migrants. Genius musician who crafted his own instruments from scraps. Noticed by an inventor from the Spires, taken as a pupil. Joined same University as Vicious, became friends. Parted ways after graduation. Joined a band called Los Sin Voz, speaking against the Cathedral. Band members were attacked and killed. Feels deep regret for not being there. Reached out to Vicious, joined him in creating the Misfits.

[ Goals ]

Fight for the people with no voice. Never let what happened to Los Sin Voz happen again.

[ Fears ]

Losing another family and not being there to stop it.
