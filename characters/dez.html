Mischievous, playful, chaotic but good-hearted. Cute yet confident with a relaxed gyaru attitude. Popular kid energy.

Used her love arrow on a Cathedral figure's son at a club, became wanted. Shirna sent her to the Wastes with Rizz. Met Danny fighting Cathedral soldiers, joined the Misfits.
