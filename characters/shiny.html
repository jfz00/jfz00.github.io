[ Personality ]

Kindness itself — warm, gentle, oblivious and naive. A goody two shoes who believes in true justice. Often admonishes Gelee, Cammy and Jonny for being too chaotic.

[ Backstory ]

Born in the Crown, strict family. Becoming a Holy Knight was expected. Trained under the Cathedral's care to earn the distinctive red armor. Discovered corruption and authoritarianism. Tried to confront Nathanael, was branded a heretic. Fled to the Warrens, found by Nienna who helped her. Joined the Misfits.

[ Goals ]

To erase corruption from the city.

[ Fears ]

That she'll be too powerless to apply her sense of justice.
