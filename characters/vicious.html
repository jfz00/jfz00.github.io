[ Personality ]

Nonchalant, smartass, argues a lot but reasonable. Confident laid-back swagger, knows he's the strongest in the room.

[ Backstory ]

Son of a family from the Crown, genius enginer. Met Danny during college. Built a tech company in the Spires. After the Gemstone Mines uprising, discovered corporations were using his tools to push sentient machines into slavery. Confronted corporations, framed as instigator and heretic, company seized. Moved to the Tracks, found Hardhat, saved and repaired him. Founded the Misfits with Danny and Hardhat.

[ Goals ]

To take down the Cathedral.

[ Fears ]

That the Cathedral will slowly turn this city into a dictatorship.
