[ Personality ]

A loud mouth who laughs even louder. Former rich kid with an edge. Loyal and loves her friends more than anything.

[ Backstory ]

Daughter of a rich family from the Spires. Lost her parents young because of a demon. Corporations seized her parents' assets. Fled to the Wastes, met Aura who helped her survive and learn magic. Moved to Warrens at 17, became a famous bounty hunter. 3 years later, discovered she was wanted for her parents' murder. Ran back to the Wastes, met Vicious, invited to the Misfits.

[ Goals ]

To find and kill the demon who murdered her parents.

[ Fears ]

Demons — which is why she's fighting them, to fight her own fears.
