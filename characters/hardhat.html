[ Personality ]

A man of few words. Speaks in a very straightforward, robotic manner. Calm, imposing, practical. Craves freedom for all.

[ Backstory ]

Manufactured to work in the magical gemstone mines west of Terminal 00. Forced into basic slavery despite being sentient. Organized an uprising with every robot worker. Stayed behind to fight — Hero of the Uprising. Defeated by a high-ranking Holy Knight, sent for recycling. Vicious found and repaired him. Joined the Misfits.

[ Goals ]

To help Vicious and free all robot slaves.

[ Fears ]

That machines will never get rights despite being sentient.
