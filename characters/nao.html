[ Personality ]

Very proud and serious but oblivious and literal, making her easy to tease. Fiercely loyal and caring for her friends, with a hot temper if anyone tries to mess with them. Speaks in a noble, royal manner. Always looks like she's showing contempt even though she doesn't mean to.

[ Backstory ]

Nao was a victim of experiments in Terminal 06, where researchers tried to control her blood magic. She escaped during a failure in the facility and fled to Terminal 00, finding refuge in the Wastes.

[ Goals ]

To help her friends.

[ Fears ]

Going back to being a specimen for experimentations.
