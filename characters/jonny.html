[ Backstory ]

Born in the Warrens as an orphan, mute from birth. Only friend was Snek. Found by the Ashen Line assassin guild at 13, trained by Rougebleu. 7 years later, assigned to kill Katy, defeated easily. Katy spared him, dragged him to the Misfits. Stacy and Nao cared for his wounds. Slowly became part of the gang.
